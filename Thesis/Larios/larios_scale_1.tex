\chapter{Larios Scale Results}

Larios et al. 2018 present numerical evidence for blow-up of the Euler equations from (\ref{eq:TGV}) occurring at $T^* \approx 4$. Their numerical model used parameters $L=1$ and $V_0=1$, and so the characteristic time-scale of their model is $t_c = 1$. These parameters give their model an initial seminorm $\|\bn\eta_{TGV}\|_{L^2} = \sqrt{3}\pi$ (\ref{app:tg:seminorm:1}) and initial vorticity $\|\nabla\times\eta_{TGV}\|_{L^\infty} = 4\pi$ (\ref{app:tg:vorticity:1}). 
\\~\\
Because our model uses the same domain we do not need to scale our TGV.
\\~\\
The plots below show our results for the unscaled TGV. In addition they show the comparison of our results with those of (Fig. 1b, Bustamante et al. 2012), (Fig. 2, Larios et al. 2018), and (Fig. 3, Larios et al. 2018). 

\section{Evolution of $\|\nabla\ua\|_{L^2}$}

This plot shows the evolution of the $\|\nabla\ua\|_{L^2}$ norm over time. Neither Bustamante, Brachet, or Larios show an equivalent plot but it is still worth examining. The growth in the norm strictly increases over time. Additionally, the norm at T increases as $\alpha \rightarrow 0$ which is consistent with the expected behaviour. The time-scale of the results is much faster than the Bustamante scaled model. Additionally, the growth in the seminorm slows down and appears to plateau over time.

\caption{$\|\nabla\ua\|_{L^2}$ vs $t$.}

\section{Evolution of $\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}$}

This plot shows the evolution of the $\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}$ norm over time for each value of $\alpha$. This plot is compared to Fig. 1b in Bustamente et al. 2012. Additionally, we include our reproduction of Fig 1b for Euler. The plot shows that the evolution of the vorticity for the unscaled Euler-Voigt model does not converge to the plot in Fig 1b. Therefore, when the initial TGV is scaled to $\|\bn\eta_{TGV}\|_{L^2} = \sqrt{3}\pi$ the solution to Euler-Voigt does not match the results of Bustamante et al. 2012.

\section{Conclusion}

The results of our scaled Euler-Voigt model show that it does not match the time-scale of the Bustamante et al. model, it does match the Larios et a. model for Fig 2 but not for Fig 3.
