\chapter{Taylor Green $\hdo$ in $[0,1]^3$ domain}
\label{app:tg:seminorm:1}

The 3D Taylor Green initial condition on the domain $[0,1]^3$ is
$$\eta_{TG} = \begin{bmatrix}
           \sin(2\pi x)\cos(2\pi y)\cos(2\pi z) \\
           -\cos(2\pi x)\sin(2\pi y)\cos(2\pi z) \\
           0.
         \end{bmatrix}$$
The formula for the H1 seminorm is
$$\|\eta\|_{\hdo} = \|\nabla\eta\|_{L^2} = \left[ \sum_{i,j=1}^3 \int_{\Omega} (\partial_i\eta_j)^2 \,dx \right]^{1/2}.$$
Because the z component of $\eta_{TG}$ is zero, the partial derivative is also zero. This leaves us with six integrals to evaluate.
\begin{align*}
\sum_{i,j=1}^3 \int_{\Omega} (\partial_i\eta_j)^2 \,dx &= \int_{\Omega} (\partial_x\eta_x)^2 \, dx + \int_{\Omega} (\partial_y\eta_x)^2 \, dx \\
&+ \int_{\Omega} (\partial_z\eta_x)^2 \, dx + \int_{\Omega} (\partial_x\eta_y)^2 \, dx \\ 
&+ \int_{\Omega} (\partial_y\eta_y)^2 \, dx + \int_{\Omega} (\partial_z\eta_y)^2 \, dx.
\end{align*}
Evaluating each partial derivative
\begin{align*}
\sum_{i,j=1}^3 \int_{\Omega} (\partial_i\eta_j)^2 \,dx &= \int_{\Omega} (2\pi \cos(2\pi x)\cos(2\pi y)\cos(2\pi z))^2 \, dx \\ &+ \int_{\Omega} (-2\pi \sin(2\pi x)\sin(2\pi y)\cos(2\pi z))^2 \, dx \\
&+ \int_{\Omega} (-2\pi \sin(2\pi x)\cos(2\pi y)\sin(2\pi z))^2 \, dx \\ &+ \int_{\Omega} (2\pi \sin(2\pi x)\sin(2\pi y)\cos(2\pi z))^2 \, dx \\ 
&+ \int_{\Omega} (-2\pi \cos(2\pi x)\cos(2\pi y)\cos(2\pi z))^2 \, dx \\ &+ \int_{\Omega} (2\pi \cos(2\pi x)\sin(2\pi y)\sin(2\pi z))^2 \, dx.
\end{align*}
Evaluating each integral
$$\sum_{i,j=1}^3 \int_{\Omega} (\partial_i\eta_j)^2 \,dx = \frac{\pi^2}{2} + \frac{\pi^2}{2} + \frac{\pi^2}{2} + \frac{\pi^2}{2} + \frac{\pi^2}{2} + \frac{\pi^2}{2}= 3\pi^2. $$
\\
Substituting into the formula for the H1 seminorm
\\
$$\|\eta\|_{\hdo} = \left[ 3\pi^2 \right]^{1/2} = \sqrt{3}\pi.$$
