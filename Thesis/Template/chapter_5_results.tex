\begin{equation}
\sup_{t\in[0,T^*]}\|\nabla\ua(t)\|_{L^2} \approx \mathcal{O}(\alpha^p),

for $T^* > 0$ sufficiently large and for some power $p$. From Theorem (XINYU THEOREM), if there exists a finite time $T^*>0$ such that
\begin{equation}
\limsup_{\alpha\rightarrow 0}\left( \alpha^2\sup_{t\in[0,T^*]}\|\nabla\ua(t)\|_{L^2}^2\right) > 0.
\end{equation}
then $\u$ develops a singularity in the interval $[0,T^*]$. From this ansatz, in order for singularity formation to occur, we must have $p\leq -1$.
